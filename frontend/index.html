Technology

The technology behind the concept.

EcoExtract combines a digital dashboard, AI-vision concepts, telemetry and data visualisation to create a smarter e-waste workflow.

The concept uses computer-vision logic to identify an incoming electronic item and associate it with a suitable material category before routing it to the appropriate collection bin.
Once a category is identified, the dashboard can represent an automated sorting decision and communicate the destination stream for the item.
EcoExtract records collection information and translates it into simple environmental metrics such as material value, waste diverted and potential impact.

About My Website — EcoExtract

EcoExtract is an AI-vision-powered concept for smarter electronic waste management and resource recovery.

The goal of this project is to make e-waste management more organised, data-driven, and environmentally responsible. By combining AI-based vision concepts with a digital monitoring dashboard, EcoExtract aims to support better waste segregation, collection tracking, and the recovery of valuable materials from discarded electronics.

Why EcoExtract?

Electronic waste contains materials that can potentially be recovered and reused, while improper disposal can create environmental challenges. EcoExtract explores how technology can make the collection and segregation process more visible, organised and data-driven.

From Waste to Data

Instead of treating e-waste as a single category, EcoExtract represents it as a collection of measurable material streams. This makes it easier to visualise what is collected, where it goes and what potential value it represents.

My Mission

My mission is to explore how technology and artificial intelligence can help solve environmental challenges. Through EcoExtract, I aim to contribute to a future where electronic waste is managed more responsibly and valuable resources can be recovered more efficiently.
